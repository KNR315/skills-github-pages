An intentional pause

The rhythm of returning

A planning framework for generally healthy adults. Your chosen time includes warm-up and rest; it is never a target for continuous stretching.

01. Arrive

Notice your energy and choose one comfortable movement to revisit later. Settle with ordinary breathing; no breath-holding.

02. Warm gently

Begin with easy walking or marching. Give yourself time to feel ready before exploring a stretch.

03. Explore

Select an upper-body, lower-body, or whole-body theme. Use small, unforced movements. Your map is a personal intention, not an assessment.

04. Rest

Build in supported sitting or lying, water as desired, and pauses between movements. Longer time does not mean more intensity.

05. Revisit

Repeat your original easy movement without pushing farther. Decide whether to repeat, simplify, or stop.

06. Carry it forward

Choose one gentle practice for another day. Persistent pain or an injury needs assessment by an appropriate licensed professional.

Your pace is the priority

This is general education, not individualized rehabilitation. Stay clothed and move yourself; no one needs to push or position you. Stop for pain, dizziness, numbness, tingling, or unusual weakness. Recent surgery, injury, pregnancy complications, or a significant medical condition may require individualized advice before starting.
