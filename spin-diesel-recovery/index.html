The Sequence

Every phase is an offering, never a requirement. You design your session below; shorter bookings focus only on your chosen priorities.

I. Dorsal Chain

Prone · 15 Min

  • Broad Effleurage: Sweeping somatic warm-up for the posterior chain.
  • Deep Stripping: Glacial pacing up the latissimus dorsi.
  • Ischemic Compression: Melting the infraspinatus and upper traps.

II. Grounding

Prone · 10 Min

  • Gluteal Compression: Broad forearm pressure, always draped.
  • Hamstring Pétrissage: Slow, downward rhythmic kneading.
  • Calf Release: Unbinding the gastrocnemius from toe-point fatigue.

III. Hip Mechanics

Supine · 10 Min

  • Inner-Thigh Flush: Flat compression kept strictly above the knee crease. Opt-in.
  • Diaphragmatic Release: Breath-assisted work at the iliacus. Opt-in.

IV. Anterior Open

Supine · 10 Min

  • Pectoral Sweep: Opening the front shoulder line.
  • Pec Minor Anchor: Static pressure beneath the clavicle. Opt-in.
  • Subscapularis Hold: Guided ribcage release. Opt-in.

V. The Grip Base

Supine · 15 Min

  • Pin-and-Stretch: Active receiver movement targeting forearm flexors.
  • Epicondyle Relief: Soothing the origins of golfer's elbow.
  • Thenar Friction: Cross-fiber unbinding of the thumb pad.

Integration

Hydrate deeply and avoid maximum-effort training for 24 hours. Light structural soreness is normal; we will stay in touch to ensure your recovery is seamless.

The Philosophy of Consent

  • Enthusiastic Opt-In. Intrusive areas are skipped by default. "Ask me first" means we will discuss the touch in real-time, and wait for your explicit permission.

  • Absolute Control. Say "pause", "skip", or simply raise a hand. The technique ends immediately, without question or explanation required.

  • Your Sanctuary. You decide your level of undress. Only the specific area being addressed is uncovered. Privacy is sacred.
